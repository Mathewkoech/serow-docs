# Profit & Loss Statement

## Overview
The Profit & Loss (P&L) statement, also known as Income Statement, shows your business's revenues, costs, and expenses over a specific period. It provides a clear picture of profitability by calculating the difference between income and expenses to show net profit or loss.

## Accessing Profit & Loss
1. Click **Accounts** in main navigation
2. Select **Profit & Loss** from sidebar

![Profit & Loss Statement]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/profit-loss.png)

## Statement Structure

### Summary Cards (Top Section)

The statement displays three key metrics at the top:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Income (KES)** | Total revenue from all income sources |
| **Expense (KES)** | Total costs and expenses for the period |
| **Profit Before Tax (KES)** | Net income (Income - Expense) |

**Color Indicators:**
- 🟢 Green card - Income (positive)
- 🔴 Red card - Expense (outgoing)
- 🔵 Blue card - Profit Before Tax (can be positive or negative)

### Income Section

**Income** - Shows all revenue streams:

#### Income
- **Sales of Product Income** - Revenue from product sales
- Shows individual income amounts
- Subtotal of all income sources

#### Other Income
- Additional revenue not from primary operations
- Interest earned, gains on asset sales, etc.
- Subtotal of other income

**Total Income:** Sum of all income sources

### Expense Section

**Expense** - Shows all business costs:

#### Cost of Sales
- **Cost of sales** - Direct costs of producing goods sold
- Includes: Raw materials, direct labor, manufacturing costs
- Subtotal of cost of sales

#### Expenses
- Operating expenses
- Administrative costs
- Marketing expenses
- Salaries and wages
- Utilities
- Rent
- Subtotal of all expenses

#### Other Expense
- Non-operating expenses
- Interest paid, losses on asset disposal
- One-time or extraordinary expenses
- Subtotal of other expenses

**Total Expense:** Sum of all costs

### Profit Calculation

```
Profit Before Tax = Total Income - Total Expense
```

**Example from screenshot:**
- Income: KES 1,112.45
- Expense: KES 4,430.26
- Profit Before Tax: KES -3,317.81 (Loss)

## Filters & Controls

### Top Filter Bar

**Cost Center**
- Filter by department or cost center
- View P&L for specific business units
- Dropdown selection

**Date Range (Calendar Icon)**
- Select reporting period
- Common periods: Month, Quarter, Year
- Custom date range selection
- As of specific date

**Refresh Button**
- Reload statement with latest data
- Updates all calculations

**Export Button**
- Download P&L statement
- Available formats: Excel, PDF, CSV
- Includes all detail rows and subtotals

## Statement Features

### Expandable/Collapsible Sections

Each section can be expanded or collapsed:
- **▼** Expanded - Shows detail accounts
- **▶** Collapsed - Shows only section totals

Click on section headers to toggle view.

### Drill-Down Capability

Click on any account line to:
1. View underlying transactions
2. See transaction details
3. Access source documents
4. Verify calculations

### Color Coding

**Green background:** Income section
**Red background:** Expense section
**Account-level detail:** Individual line items shown in white

## Common P&L Scenarios

### Profitable Business
```
Income:        KES 500,000
Expense:       KES 350,000
Profit:        KES 150,000 (Positive)
```

### Loss-Making Business
```
Income:        KES 1,112.45
Expense:       KES 4,430.26
Loss:          KES -3,317.81 (Negative)
```

### Break-Even
```
Income:        KES 100,000
Expense:       KES 100,000
Profit:        KES 0.00 (Zero)
```

## Month-End Process

### Step-by-Step

1. **Record All Transactions**
   - Enter all sales
   - Record all expenses
   - Complete journal entries
   - Reconcile accounts

2. **Review P&L**
   - Generate statement
   - Check for errors
   - Verify unusual items
   - Drill down if needed

3. **Analyze Results**
   - Calculate ratios
   - Compare to targets
   - Identify trends
   - Document findings

4. **Export and Share**
   - Export to Excel/PDF
   - Share with management
   - Prepare commentary
   - File for records

## Key Financial Ratios

### Gross Profit Margin
```
(Revenue - Cost of Sales) / Revenue × 100%
```

### Net Profit Margin
```
Profit Before Tax / Revenue × 100%
```

### Operating Expense Ratio
```
Operating Expenses / Revenue × 100%
```

## Tips & Troubleshooting

**Tip: Period Comparison**
- Run P&L for multiple periods
- Export each to Excel
- Create side-by-side comparison
- Calculate growth rates

**Tip: Budget vs Actual**
- Set up budgets in Budget module
- Compare actual P&L to budget
- Focus on significant variances
- Investigate root causes

**Issue: Income showing as zero**
- Check date range selected
- Verify sales transactions recorded
- Confirm fiscal year settings
- Review account classifications

**Issue: Expenses seem incorrect**
- Verify expense transactions
- Check account mappings
- Review journal entries
- Confirm opening balances

**Issue: Statement doesn't balance**
- Reconcile all accounts
- Check for unposted entries
- Verify tax calculations
- Contact support if needed

**Issue: Missing accounts**
- Check cost center filter
- Verify account setup
- Review account type classification
- Ensure accounts are active
