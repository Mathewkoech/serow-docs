# Customers Analytics

Understand your customer base with detailed analytics on customer sales behavior, inquiries, and account ledgers.

## Customer Sales Analytics

### Overview

The Customer Sales Analytics section shows detailed sales information for each customer, helping you understand purchasing patterns and customer value.

### Accessing Customer Analytics

1. Go to **Analytics** > **Customers** > **Customer Analytics**
2. View list of all customers
3. See sales metrics for each customer
4. Filter and sort as needed

### Customer Sales Metrics

For each customer, view:

- **Customer Name**: Business or individual name
- **Total Sales**: Lifetime sales amount
- **Average Transaction**: Mean purchase value
- **Total Items Purchased**: Quantity of items
- **Account Balance**: Amount owed or credit

### Sorting Customers

#### Sort by Frequency

1. Click **Sort** button
2. Select **Transaction Count** (most to least)
3. View most frequent customers
4. Identify loyal customers

### Filtering Customers

#### Filter by Date Range

1. Click **Filter** button
2. Select **Date Range**
3. Choose period for sales analysis
4. Click **Ok**

### Analyzing Customer Value

#### High-Value Customers

- Total sales > threshold
- Frequent purchases
- Consistent buying patterns
- Action: Provide VIP service, special offers

#### Growing Customers

- Increasing sales trend
- Increasing purchase frequency
- Expanding product range
- Action: Nurture relationship, offer loyalty program

#### At-Risk Customers

- Decreasing sales trend
- Longer time since last purchase
- Reduced purchase frequency
- Action: Reach out, offer incentives, understand concerns

#### New Customers

- Recent first purchase
- Limited transaction history
- Action: Welcome, encourage repeat purchases, gather feedback

<div class="callout tip">
  <div class="callout-title">✅ Best Practice</div>
  <p>Focus retention efforts on high-value customers. A small percentage of customers often generate the majority of revenue.</p>
</div>

## Customer Inquiries

### Overview

The Customer Inquiries section tracks customer questions, requests, and support tickets, helping you monitor customer engagement and satisfaction.

### Accessing Customer Inquiries

1. Navigate to **Analytics** > **Customers** > **Customer Inquiry**
2. View list of all customer inquiries
4. Filter by date, or customer

### Filtering Inquiries

#### Filter by Date Range

1. Click **Filter** button
2. Select **Date Range**
3. Choose period
4. Click **Ok**
5. View inquiries from selected period

## Customer Ledgers

### Overview

The Customer Ledgers section shows detailed account information for each customer, including transactions, balances, and payment history.

### Accessing Customer Ledgers

1. Go to **Analytics** > **Customers** > **Customers Ledgers**
2. Select a customer from the list
3. View their complete account ledger
4. See all transactions and balance

### Ledger Information

For each customer, view:

- **Customer Name**: Account holder
- **Account Number**: Unique identifier
- **Opening Balance**: Balance at start of period
- **Total Debits**: Total sales/charges
- **Total Credits**: Total payments/returns
- **Closing Balance**: Current balance
- **Account Status**: Active, Suspended, Closed
- **Credit Limit**: Maximum credit allowed
- **Days Sales Outstanding**: Average payment time

### Ledger Transactions

View detailed transaction list:

- **Date**: Transaction date
- **Reference**: Invoice or receipt number
- **Description**: Transaction details
- **Debit**: Amount charged (sales)
- **Credit**: Amount paid (payment/return)
- **Balance**: Running balance after transaction
- **Due Date**: Payment due date (if applicable)

### Filtering Ledger Transactions

#### Filter by Date Range

1. Click **Filter** button
2. Select **Date Range**
3. Choose period
4. Click **Ok**
5. View transactions in selected period

#### Filter by Transaction Type

1. Click **Filter** button
2. Select **Transaction Type**:
   - Sales/Invoices
   - Payments
   - Returns/Credit Notes
   - Adjustments
3. Click **Ok**
4. View specific transaction types

#### Filter by Amount Range

1. Click **Filter** button
2. Select **Amount Range**
3. Set minimum and maximum
4. Click **Ok**
5. View transactions within range

### Account Analysis

#### Outstanding Balance

- Amount customer owes
- Days overdue (if applicable)
- Action: Follow up for payment

#### Payment Patterns

- Average payment time
- On-time payment percentage
- Late payment frequency
- Action: Adjust credit terms if needed

#### Credit Utilization

- Current balance vs credit limit
- Percentage of credit used
- Available credit remaining
- Action: Approve credit increase or restrict further credit

#### Transaction Frequency

- Number of transactions per period
- Average transaction value
- Trend over time
- Action: Identify changes in buying behavior

<div class="callout warning">
  <div class="callout-title">⚠️ Collection Priority</div>
  <p>Focus collection efforts on accounts with high outstanding balances and long overdue periods. Consider payment plans for large overdue amounts.</p>
</div>

## Exporting Customer Data

### Export to PDF

1. Click **Export** button
2. Select **PDF Format**
3. Choose what to include:
   - Customer list
   - Sales analytics
   - Inquiries summary
   - Ledger details
4. Click **Generate PDF**
5. File downloads

### Export to Excel

1. Click **Export** button
2. Select **Excel Format**
3. Choose data to export:
   - Customer sales data
   - Inquiry list
   - Ledger transactions
4. Click **Download Excel**
5. Open in spreadsheet application

## Tips & Troubleshooting

### Customer Data Not Showing?

- Verify customers exist in system
- Check date range includes customer activity
- Ensure user has permission to view customers
- Try refreshing the page

### Inquiry Status Not Updating?

- Verify inquiry is assigned to staff member
- Check if status was properly saved
- Ensure correct inquiry is selected
- Try updating status again

### Ledger Balance Incorrect?

- Verify all transactions are recorded
- Check for pending or draft transactions
- Review transaction details for accuracy
- Ensure correct customer is selected
