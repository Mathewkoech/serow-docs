# Customers Module

## Overview

The Customers module allows you to manage your customer base, track their purchase history, and maintain contact information.

## Key Features

- **Customer Profiles**: Store detailed information about your customers.
- **Purchase History**: View past transactions and items purchased.
- **Account Management**: Track balances, credits, and payments.
- **Groups & Categories**: Organize customers for targeted marketing.

## Accessing Customers

1. Navigate to the **Customers** module in the main menu.
2. View the list of all registered customers.
3. Click on a customer name to view their full profile.
